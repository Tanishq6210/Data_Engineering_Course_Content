Question 1 – Identify Performance Bottlenecks

The following query is taking a long time to execute:

SELECT *
FROM orders
WHERE YEAR(order_date) = 2025
ORDER BY total_amount DESC;
Tasks

a) Identify at least three performance bottlenecks in the query.

b) Rewrite the query to improve its performance.

c) Explain why your optimized query is faster.

Question 2 – Reduce Data Movement

The business team only needs the customer name and order amount for customers from Bangalore.

An existing developer wrote:

SELECT *
FROM customers c
JOIN orders o
ON c.customer_id = o.customer_id
WHERE c.city = 'Bangalore';
Tasks

a) Rewrite the query to reduce unnecessary data movement.

b) Explain which optimization techniques you applied.

c) Why is selecting only required columns considered a good practice?
