Part A – Table Creation 
Create a table named movies with the following requirements:

Column	Requirement
movie_id	Integer, Primary Key
title	Maximum 150 characters, cannot be NULL
genre	Maximum 50 characters, cannot be NULL
duration	Integer, should be greater than 30 minutes
language	Maximum 30 characters
release_date	Date
ticket_price	Decimal number, must be greater than 0
available	Boolean
created_at	Timestamp
imdb_rating	Decimal number between 0 and 10 (inclusive)

Apply all appropriate constraints (PRIMARY KEY, NOT NULL, CHECK).

Part B – Insert Data 
Insert at least 6 movie records into the table.

Your data should include:
Different genres
Different languages
Both available and unavailable movies
Different ticket prices
Different IMDb ratings


Part C – Update Records 
Perform the following updates:

Increase the ticket price of one movie by ₹50.
Change the availability of one movie from TRUE to FALSE.
Update the language of one movie.


Part D – Delete Records
Delete one movie whose ticket price is less than ₹200.

Part E – Constraint Testing
Execute the following operations and observe the database error message.

Insert a movie with an existing movie_id.
Insert a movie without a title.
Insert a movie with a negative ticket price.
Insert a movie with a duration of 20 minutes.
Insert a movie with an IMDb rating of 12.
