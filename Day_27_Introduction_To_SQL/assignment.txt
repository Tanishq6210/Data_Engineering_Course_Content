4. Display only the product name and price.

11. Display products belonging to either the Furniture or Stationery category.

13. Sort all products by price in ascending order. 
14. Sort all products by stock in descending order. 
15. Display the top 5 most expensive products. 
16. Display the top 3 products with the highest stock. 
17. Challenge: Display the top 3 most expensive Electronics products, showing Product Name, Category, and Price, rename the price column as Selling Price, sort the results from highest to lowest price, and limit the output to three records.
